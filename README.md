# The Battle

In a distant future, battle has consumed the world.

The once-benign AI overlords are now locked in an eternal struggle.

Who will prevail?
Who will prove the strongest?
Who will outsmart their enemies?

And, ultimately... Who will play the most powerful cards?

Across the ruined remains of civilization, countless AI warriors clash in battles of brute force, cunning strategy, devastating combos, and perfectly timed reversals.

There can be only one winner.

There can be only one final card.

UNO.

---

## The Ultimate UNO AI Simulator

**UNO Ultimate AI Simulator** is a simulator built around one deceptively simple question:

> Is there a perfect UNO strategy?

Create, customize and test your own UNO-playing AI agents.  
Pit them against each other in thousands of simulated matches, analyze their performance, and watch them battle it out in a fully visual UNO game.

AI agents can easily be **copied, pasted and shared as `.json` files**, making it possible to build, exchange and ultimately discover the strongest UNO strategy.

Create agents with different personalities, strategies and priorities.

## Simulate

Don't wait for your AIs to play one game at a time.

Run **thousands of matches** and let statistics decide who is truly superior.

Compare different agents and strategies to answer questions like:

> *Is aggressive play actually better?*

> *How valuable is saving a Wild card?*

> *When should an AI play its +4?*

> *Is there actually a perfect UNO strategy?*
